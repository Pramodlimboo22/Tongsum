2nd Annual Tongsum Village Tourism Festival 2026

Celebrating Culture, Heritage and Village Tourism

Date: 1st to 3rd December 2026

Festival Theme and Objectives

  • Preserve heritage and tradition to promote sustainable and responsible tourism.
  • Offer solemn prayers for the victims of the devastating landslides in Rimbi and the tragic river floods in Nepal.
